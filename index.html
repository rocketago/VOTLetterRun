HOW TO PLAY

Swipe left / right to change lane. Swipe up to jump. Arrow keys and space work too.

Obstacles scatter what you are carrying — every turnstile, cart and gate costs you pages and lets him close.

Loose pages on the floor go back into the folder.

Doorways open in the outer lanes. Run through one and he gets shown to a private room for six seconds while you double your speed.

The proximity bar is the clock. When it fills, he has you.
