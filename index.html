HOW TO PLAY

Swipe left / right to change lane. Swipe up to jump. Arrow keys and space work too.

Recalled food scatters what you are carrying — every crate of romaine, beef and cantaloupe costs you bags and lets him close.

Loose bags on the floor go back into your arms.

Boosters turn up on the shelves. Take one and you get six seconds at double speed while he drops a long way back.

He stops to read an ingredients label now and then. That is free ground — use it.

The proximity bar is the clock. When it fills, he has you.
